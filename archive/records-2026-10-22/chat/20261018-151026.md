I can't confirm it. The site says balanced, but the rolls we recorded don't behave like balanced dice, and I ran a stricter test to be sure.

**The strict test:** colonist's balanced dice, and any scheme like it, draw rolls from a deck of the 36 dice pairs. So early in a game a total can't come up more often than the deck holds it: a 2 or 12 at most once, a 7 at most six times, within the first 23 rolls (colonist reshuffles after 23 draws). That is a hard limit, not a statistic.

| First 23 rolls of each game | Games breaking the deck limit |
|---|---|
| OpenHex, observed | 49 of 55 |
| Plain dice, simulated | 82% |
| Any 36-card deck | 0% |

So whatever OpenHex calls "Balanced" for 1v1, it is not a deck like colonist's. Either its balancing is far weaker, so weak that our games can't tell it from plain dice, or the setting isn't applied in bot games. Our 4,429 rolls can't separate those two.
