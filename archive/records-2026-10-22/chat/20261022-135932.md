**E54 vs the regular net, in short:** same architecture, same file format, same search at play. The difference is how it was trained.

**The regular net (rounds 73, 189, 208):** AlphaZero style. Self-play games at 200 simulations a move, the net learns to copy its own search's move choice and to predict the game result. Each round is about 6,400 games. The teacher is the net's own search, which E51 showed is barely ahead of the raw net, so progress is slow (that's the fixed point you pointed at on Oct 19).

**E54:** policy gradient (PPO), no search in training. It started from round 73 and learned straight from win/loss, playing raw net against raw net at 0.1 to 0.2 seconds a game, so about 60x the games per core. Each step is 20,000 games: half against itself, half against a pool of older nets (rounds 73, 66, 13) and the people model. A KL penalty keeps it close to round 73 so the policy stays a usable prior inside search. Step 30 was run 1's stop; E83 continued it to step 60 on e54-vm, and step 50 was the best.

**What it bought:** step 30 beat round 73 by 57% raw and 58% with search, and round 152 by 56%. Step 50 beat round 189 by 54% with search. Live, Tedi4960 on step 50 is at 2439 Diamond #23 this morning, the top account.

**What it costs:** its value head is worse on people's games. Every E54 step is about 0.027 worse than round 189 on the 2100+ holdout, mostly over-confident when behind. That's why it closed as a production seed (Oct 20) and lives on two accounts as an experiment, and why E85 and E88 are about fixing the value side.

**Why it matters for the loop:** it's the first proof that the net is data-limited rather than architecture-limited, and the people check in the gate (E81) came out of what it broke.
