Yes, automatically, and the whole chain already runs without anyone touching it.

1. **Retrain every round.** Before each training round the human-game set is rebuilt from the new captures, and in the background the opponent model is retrained from the current best net on people's moves. The new model replaces the old one when it scores at least as well on the held-out games. In practice it has been replaced on every refresh since Oct 13.
2. **Ship within 5 minutes.** The bot sync copies the model file to Petra's VM on its 5-minute cycle.
3. **Reload between games.** Petra's runner checks the file's timestamp after every game and restarts its search with the new model when it has changed. No manual restart, no mid-game change.

How much it has improved: the model predicted 64.5% of people's moves on Oct 13 and predicts 67.4% today, with the data growing from about 1,500 games to 3,800. That is the model getting better at guessing what people do. Whether that wins games is the open question from E2, which found no difference over 483 games with the earlier model.
