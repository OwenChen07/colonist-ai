**E54 in plain terms.** Normal training plays slow games with full search against itself, then teaches the net to copy what the search chose. E54 skips the search. The net plays very fast games on its own, about a tenth of a second each, against a mix of its older versions and the people model, and learns directly from which moves led to wins. A leash keeps it close to round 73 so it can't drift into nonsense. Because the games are so cheap it plays 20,000 a step, 1.2 million games across the 60 steps so far. The hope was that learning from a mixed crowd of opponents, including a people-like one, would generalize to people better than self-play does.

**What it has shown.** Its nets do beat our production nets with search: step 30 beat round 152 by 56%, step 50 beats round 189 by 54%. That is real. But two things haven't moved:
- Raw strength is flat from step 10 to 60, between 55% and 59% against round 73 with no trend. Step 50's 58.6% may be a lucky peak.
- It predicts strong people's games worse than every production net, by a steady 0.03, unchanged from step 10 to 60.
