Two epochs per round, over the 18-round window of 12 million positions (set with E37 last night). More would not help; this has been tested twice.

- **E31 (Oct 18):** going from 4 epochs down to 1 changed nothing. Validation loss and the gate score stayed the same, and training got four times faster. We kept 1, then went to 2 with the bigger window.
- **E46 (last night):** one long run of 4 epochs over the whole window. Validation loss stopped moving after epoch 2 (policy 0.867, value 0.405 on epochs 2, 3 and 4), while the training loss kept falling. That is memorising, not learning. Its gate was 50.0% over 6,000 games against round 73, no gain.

There is also a hidden multiplier. Training continues from the previous round's net, and a position stays in the window for 18 rounds, so each position is seen about 36 times over its life. The net is already saturated on the data it has.

So epochs are not the lever. The net learns everything in the data within two passes; what it needs is different data, which is why today's bets are the exact-hand human moves (E56, in production from round 125), 800-simulation targets (E51), and research's plan for a different training method at 2:30 PM.
