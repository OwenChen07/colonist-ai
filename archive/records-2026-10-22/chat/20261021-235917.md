Held-out real games, not the people model.

The number comes from the human holdout: captured ranked games the bots actually played on colonist.io, set aside from training, filtered to opponents rated 2100 or above by their recorded rating (243 games, about 9,600 positions). For each position the net states a win chance from the bot's seat, and the error is the squared gap between that and how the real game ended. So "0.646 vs round 189's 0.619" means E54's nets are worse at judging who is winning in real games against strong people. Lower is better.

The people model plays no part in it. The people model is only used as an opponent in E54's training games and, since this afternoon, in the promotion gate. The two are separate yardsticks: one asks "can you beat an imitation of people," the other "do you understand real games against people."
