Probably yes, but check first; it takes one minute.

**Why:** the trial quota is 12 vCPUs and we use all 12. Upgrading usually raises it, but Google doesn't publish the number (community reports say about 24 per region for a new paid account, and sometimes it stays low). We want 12 + 40 = 52, so the odds are you need a request anyway. Spot VMs count against the normal **CPUs** quota unless you have a separate **Preemptible CPUs** quota, so requesting both keeps things simple.

**Do this after the upgrade:**
1. In the console: IAM & Admin → Quotas & System Limits → filter *Compute Engine API*, region *us-central1*. Or from the Mac: `gcloud compute regions describe us-central1 --project colonist-ai-selfplay` and look at the `CPUS` and `PREEMPTIBLE_CPUS` limits.
2. If `CPUS` is already 64 or more: done, no request.
3. If not: tick CPUs and Preemptible CPUs, Edit, request 64 each. Small requests are often approved in minutes; worst case about two business days. Trial accounts can't request at all, which is another reason to upgrade first.

Either way, send me the two numbers and I'll size the VMs to fit.

Sources: [Compute Engine quotas](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/quotas), [Spot VMs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs/instances/spot), [community thread on trial quota requests](https://discuss.google.dev/t/compute-engine-update-quote-free-trial/175450)
