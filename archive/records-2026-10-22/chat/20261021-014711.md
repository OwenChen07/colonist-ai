The fast ones are the likely source of what you saw. About a third of all moves are forced, with only one legal action, nearly all rolls and end-turns. Those skip the search and go out in 4 to 20 milliseconds after the prompt arrives. So the bot rolls or ends its turn before colonist's own animation has finished, and a move that lands mid-animation is exactly where a site glitch would show.

The fix is cheap: a floor of about one second on forced moves, with a little randomness so it does not look mechanical. It costs about 20 seconds a game and nothing in strength.
