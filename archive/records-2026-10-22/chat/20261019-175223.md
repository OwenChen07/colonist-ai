**What explains the seat split, probably:**
- Moving first is an edge in 1v1 Catan by itself. On OpenHex, where bots play bots, the first seat wins 52% site-wide, so a few points of the gap are just the game.
- The rest, about 5 points, is us playing the second seat badly. On OpenHex the pattern was clear: as second seat we lose the expansion race, with the opponent taking Longest Road in 19 of 21 losses, while the opening pick itself was fine.
