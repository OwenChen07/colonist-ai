**What E16 is:** The bot's neural net predicts two things for each position: which move to play (policy) and who's going to win (value). Right now the value is trained only against the real result of the game, 1 or 0. But early in a Catan game that result is mostly dice luck, so the net is learning noise. The value error on human games is high in the opening (0.89) and that hasn't moved in weeks.

The fix, used by Lc0 (the chess engine) and in a 2021 paper: train the value against a blend of the final result and the search's own estimate at that position, which is steadier. The training code already has this option (`--value-mix`); nothing uses it yet.

**The experiment:** retrain round 67's net four times with different blend strengths (0 as control, 0.3, 0.5, 1.0), then measure the value error on human games. If the best one cuts the error by at least 0.01 without hurting the move prediction, it gets one real training round after E14's verdict.

**What the decision is:** start now, or wait until E14's verdict (round 72, about two days). It runs on your PC's GPU, but only while the training loop is in a match or benchmark and the GPU is free, the same way E11b ran. Production is untouched either way.
