"Complete" is the ranked runner's normal end-of-batch state, not a problem.

Each bot account runs in batches of 50 ranked games. When a batch ends, the runner writes "complete" and exits, and the service starts it again a few seconds later, which shows as "starting" and then the next game. bot4 finished a batch around 7:36 PM, restarted, and was back in a game by 7:43 PM (turn 54 of a live game when I looked). Its day is 65-47.

The states you'll see on the site: starting, queueing, playing, complete, and needs_attention. Only the last one means something is wrong, and that one pings you.

One side note from its log: bot4's last game before the restart was against one of our own accounts, so it was dropped from the training data, which is the intended handling.
