One change, and it is the likely cause: **E59, softer self-play move targets**, on since 12:14 AM today.

**What it is.** The search's root target was almost one-hot on the move with the higher search value. Two independent 400-sim searches of the same position disagree on the top move about 10% of the time, so the net was being trained to fit contradictory one-hot answers and could only learn a blur. E59 halves the sharpness of that target (target scale 0.5 instead of 1.0), so the target keeps most of the search's information with a third of the noise. Research measured it offline yesterday afternoon, then training switched it on at the same restart that undid E51 (800 simulations back to 400, which had shown no effect). Nothing else in self-play or training changed.

**What happened.** Round 142 was the first round played with it. Rounds 142 to 147 scored about 50.4% against round 73 (the old best), round 150 scored 51.4% over 8,000 games and promoted at 8:28 AM, the first new best in 77 rounds. Round 152 then beat round 150 at 51.0% over 8,000 and promoted at 9:59 AM. Rounds 153 and 154 scored 49.8% against 152, normal right after a new best. Live value error improved from 0.570 to 0.564, the best yet.

**How sure.** It is the only change, and the rounds trained mostly on E59 games are the ones that promoted, so it is the best explanation. But the pooled gain is small (rounds 142 to 150 at 50.5% against 49.9% before) and two promotions could partly be luck after a long dry spell. It stays on, and we revert only if rounds 151 to 156 pool under 48% against round 152 or live error goes above 0.575. Neither is close.
