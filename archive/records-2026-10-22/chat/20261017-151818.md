Not by itself. Per Google's own FAQ:

- **Upgrading charges nothing up front.** Your remaining credit (CAD 351 as of Oct 12) stays on the account and keeps paying first.
- **Your card is only charged for usage beyond the credit.** On the trial, running out of credit just stops everything; after upgrading, it bills the card instead. That's the one real difference.
- The trial also ends by itself at 90 days from signup. If that's close, upgrading is the only way to keep the coordinator running anyway. Worth checking the date on the Billing overview page.

**At our planned spend** (about CAD 15-20 a day for 40 spot vCPUs, plus the existing 12 vCPUs, maybe CAD 10 a day), 13 days comes to roughly CAD 300-400. That's close to the credit, so it's possible to run slightly past it in the last days. Two protections:
1. Set a **budget alert** in Billing → Budgets at, say, CAD 300 and CAD 340, so you get an email before the credit is gone.
2. I'll have the worker's GCP plan include a stop command, so the extra VMs can be shut off in one line, and the orchestrator can watch spend against the credit figure in `data/credits.json` (you update it with `scripts/credits.py --gcp <amount>` from the Mac).

So: safe to upgrade, as long as you set the alert and we keep the extra VMs to the planned size. Want to go ahead?
