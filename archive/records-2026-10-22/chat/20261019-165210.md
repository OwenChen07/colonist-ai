**How plus addressing works.** Gmail ignores everything between a plus sign and the @ in an address. Mail sent to owenchenyp+bot8@gmail.com lands in your normal inbox, with no setup on your side. Discord, like most sites, treats the whole string as a distinct address, so it counts as a new account even though it is your same mailbox. Discord accepts plus addresses; it does not block them the way some sites block throwaway domains. You can see which account a mail was for by looking at the "to" field, and a Gmail filter on "to: +bot8" can label them.
