The rules look the same as colonist 1v1 ranked: 15 points to win, discard over 9 cards, friendly robber, balanced dice, bank and port trades only. Our engine already has all of these, so no rules gap so far.
The bot checks the game about once a second to see if it's its turn. Each move has a 60-second clock, and a missed move cancels the game, so the client will always send something in time.
