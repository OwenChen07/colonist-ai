Tested bigger self-play parts on the same 32 games, all three side by side under the same load. 8-game parts (now): 14.30 s per game. 16-game parts: 13.77 (3.7% faster). 32-game parts: 13.33 (6.8% faster). Games and moves were identical every way, so play is unchanged. Bigger parts alone aren't worth it, because the round's last parts would finish later and eat the gain. Next: a worker that keeps about 16 games going and starts a new one as each ends, which should get close to the 32-game speed without the longer tail.
