Landed a change that makes promotion matches finish faster. Each 1000-game block waited on its slowest host: in round 80 the typical host took about 318 s but the slowest 407 s, so blocks took 6.8 min. Now the games sit in one shared queue and each host gets a new game as soon as it finishes one, so all hosts end about together. Results are unchanged (tested). Expected: about 5.4 min per 1000 games, roughly 20% shorter matches. It goes live when training restarts at round 81's match (already scheduled). Next: check rounds 81-82's timing.
