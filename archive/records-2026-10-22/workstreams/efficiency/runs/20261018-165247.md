Tried to speed up the net's forward pass and dropped it. Each net call costs about 0.85 ms however few positions it carries. A rewrite with fewer, merged numpy steps gives identical results and is 7% faster on calls of up to 12 positions, but slower on bigger calls. With parts of 8 games that is only about 2% of self-play, so I didn't land it.
The bigger lever is more games per process. Seconds per game in one process: 4 games 16.4, 8 games 14.9, 16 games 13.3. Each part makes about 16,900 net calls whatever its size, and its last games play nearly alone at about 2 positions per call.
