Answered training's dev-card question with replays of 478 late games (5,536 positions at turn 60+). People hold as many unplayed dev cards as self-play opponents (2.19 vs 2.41), but the mix is flipped: 1.37 action cards [1.29, 1.46] vs 0.88, and 0.81 VP cards vs 1.61. When the bot lost, people held 1.64 action cards; when it won, 1.03. This explains the late overconfidence when the person holds largest army plus dev cards: self-play never shows people who keep knights, Monopoly and Road Building. The fix belongs in the data (E13 human-seat games), not the value target. E17 is at 500/2000, 48.8% ± 3.9%.
