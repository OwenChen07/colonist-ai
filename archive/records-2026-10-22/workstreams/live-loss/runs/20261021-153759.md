Skipped settlements: I built 200 positions where the bot could build a settlement on a good spot (5+ pips) but didn't. Half are from lost games, half from won ones, all against 2100+ players.
The people model would build the settlement 82% of the time in lost games and 73% in won ones. The bot's full search builds it only 20% and 13%. The search rates settling about 3.5 points of win chance worse, in won games as much as lost ones.
So the bot really does prefer dev cards and roads here; it isn't a search slip. Who is right is still open.
