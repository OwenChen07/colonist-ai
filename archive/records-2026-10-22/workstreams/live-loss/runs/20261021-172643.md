People steer their roads toward ports more than the bot does, in 1,639 games against 2100+ players.
- While it has no port, 33% of the bot's mid-game roads head toward a free port. For people it's 39.5% (ranges 32-34% vs 38-41%).
- People get their first port by turn 40 in 29% of games, the bot in 13%. They reach it about 9 turns sooner. The gap holds whether the bot wins or loses.
- It's a habit gap, not yet proof that ports would win more: forcing port settlements earlier was slightly worse.
