Checked whether the bot wastes cards. It doesn't. Over 1,316 long games vs 2100+ players, rolls 20-59, it loses about the same as people to discards and bank trades, and the robber slightly favours it.
In losses it just gets 16% fewer cards (9.9 vs 11.9 per 10 rolls, 95% range 1.7-2.2 fewer).
It spends about 35% of its cards on development cards vs people's 25-29%. At the same income, more development-card spending goes with more wins (level on income: 47% vs 33%), so it isn't a leak.
