Searched every bot decision after the lead in the 17 games where it led late, lost longest road and lost: 419 decisions, 3200 sims. Deep search agrees with the move played in 78%; only 4/419 are clearly worse (all ending the turn when a port trade was better). A 3-seed check holds the trade preference (10/13 vs 3/31 in controls), but the gain is only about 1-2 points of win chance.
Verdict: these losses aren't search mistakes the net can see. They come from the late value estimate (+0.14 overconfidence) or dice. A failure set judged by the bot's own search would be circular, so late_value_set stays the benchmark. Next: dice luck in lost vs kept leads.
