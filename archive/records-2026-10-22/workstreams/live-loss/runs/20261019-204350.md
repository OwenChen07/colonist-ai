Answered live-play: how people get their last points when the bot said 20-60% at mid-game (181 ranked losses out of 236 such games, round 73).
From the bot's last hopeful move to the end, about 6 rolls, the opponent gains 4.7 points: cities 53%, victory-point cards 19%, longest road 16%, largest army 9%.
Opponents hold victory-point cards about as often whether the bot wins or loses (91% vs 89% of games), so hidden cards add at most about one point.
What separates losses from wins: largest army (58% of losses vs 18% of wins) and longest road (79% vs 45%).
So the over-hope is mostly misjudging cities and the bonus swings, not hidden cards.
