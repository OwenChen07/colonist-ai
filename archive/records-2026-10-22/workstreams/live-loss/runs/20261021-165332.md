Tested whether skipped settlements cost the bot games. From 200 positions where it could have built a settlement on a good spot but didn't, I played 6,400 games bot against bot: with the settlement forced, it won 48.5%; with the bot's real move, 50.9% (difference -2.4 points, range -4.4 to -0.4). In lost games it was -3.6 points. So skipping these settlements isn't a leak I can show, and there's no benchmark to make from it. Next: the 2 PM check on the E54 accounts.
