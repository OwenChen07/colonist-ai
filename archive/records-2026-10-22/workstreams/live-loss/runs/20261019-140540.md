Answered research's question on the new exact-hand export of people's moves. It keeps half of their moves overall, but only a third from turn 60 on, and those come from smaller hands: 1.5 cards on average against 2.2, and 3+ cards in 24% against 38% (3,817 games). The reason: colonist shows only victory-point cards at the end, so a card held unplayed to the end can't be identified. That happens in 38% of games, and there only 13% of late moves are kept. A people model trained only on this export will see too little late hoarding. Research decides the fix (weighting, or an 'unknown action card' input).
