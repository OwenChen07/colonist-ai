Counted how many of people's decisions we could use if we fill in their hidden development cards from later evidence. 3,807 train games, 331,750 decisions.
Today we use 30% (no card held). Another 20% have a hand we can know exactly, so using them takes us to 50% (two-thirds more data). Most of the gain is mid and late game: after turn 75, from 7% to 38%.
26% more are ambiguous (more than one hand fits) and 24% may hold a card that is never shown.
Knight-race games (61% of games) gain 20% too, but only 5% of them are fully known throughout.
