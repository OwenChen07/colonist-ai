Checked whether the bot under-buys development cards late in long games (1,073 games of 70+ rolls vs 2100+). It doesn't: per 10 turns after roll 60 the bot buys 3.77 vs people's 3.55 (gap +0.21, 95% range +0.01 to +0.40). It also sits on spendable cards less often than people (3.8% vs 4.8% of turns). People's late victory-point cards come from more income, not from the bot buying too few. That idea is ruled out.
