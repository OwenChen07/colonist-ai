Checked where the bot puts the robber in 1,631 games against players rated 2100+.
The bot blocks 76% of the most it could have blocked in losses and 81% in wins. People get 83-86% either way.
Most of the gap is the bot blocking its own income (17-19% of its robber moves vs people's 6%). That is what E78 already tests.
It does not go after the person's ore, wheat and sheep any less in losses than in wins (64-65% in both). People get 70-74%. Not a loss driver.
