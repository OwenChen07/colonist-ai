Cut-off second settlement study (E72, 5,215 live games, nothing live changed). We win 61% as first player and 55% as second. People put their second settlement on wood and brick about twice as often as our bot, and block our road direction a bit more often (25-33% of the time it's possible, against our 20%). It matters little: in our second-seat games we win about 6 points less when blocked and 5 more when we block, both within chance.
