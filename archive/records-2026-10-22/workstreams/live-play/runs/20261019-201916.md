Cities: does more thinking time help the bot build cities? No. On 120 positions where a city was affordable, a 12,000-simulation search picked the city as often as today's 4,800 (early 39 vs 39.5 of 60, late 42 vs 42). So skipped cities come from the net's judgment, not too little thinking, and there's no city-specific live test. Passed to training as a finding.
