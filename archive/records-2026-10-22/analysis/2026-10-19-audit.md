# Audit: our training and search against what colonist really does

Started Oct 19, 3:20 PM (openhex worker, the lead's order from the owner's 2:59 PM instruction). Read-only: findings go to the owning workstream. Scripts: `data/orchestrator/scratch/openhex/audit_*.py`.

Status: all five items done by Oct 19, 3:35 PM Toronto (some earlier times in this file were written from a clock that ran ahead).

**Summary.** The boards, dice, rules, scoring and the net's input all match colonist: no gap found in 400-4,800 games each. Three real problems, all on the live side, not in training: (1) after a robber move with nobody to rob, the bot lost its whole turn in about 1 game in 10 (fixed in 672cbcd, Oct 19); (2) about 1% of games end with the bridge stopping mid-game, recently mostly "move not confirmed", including 4 discards that colonist recorded differently from what we sent; (3) the clocks are barely used (opening settlement 21% of its 60 s, turns 11%). One open question for training: self-play and the promotion gate search far less than live (24-800 and 200 simulations against ~3,500 live); a 400-game match at 2,400 simulations would check that the gate's ranking holds at live depth.

## Clocks (item 3): colonist's timer per prompt, from 400 captures

turnState / actionState → what colonist allows:
- Setup settlement (0,1): 60 s, always a new start. Setup road (0,3): 15 s.
- Roll (1,0): 3 s, or 8 s holding a playable card; 18 s in a few cases.
- A 7's discards (1,28): 20 s, both players at once. Robber after a 7 (1,24): 15 s (18 s if the roll had 8).
- Main turn (2,0): 30 s from the roll, +5 s per build, buy or trade (no new start).
- Knight in the turn (2,24): the turn's clock, no new start. Road Building in the turn (2,30)→(2,31): the turn's clock +10 s, +10 s.
- Road Building before the roll (1,30)→(1,31): 18 s, then 28 s. Year of Plenty / Monopoly picks (1,32), (1,33), (2,32), (2,33): no new time.
- Placement modes (2,4), (2,6), (2,7): no new time.

The bridge's BUDGETS match these: no prompt found with a wrong mapping besides E58 (fixed). The bot ran out of time rarely outside finding 1: main turn 0.05% after a normal roll, roll 37 of 55,374.

Two cosmetic fixes: a road's two corners can come in either order, and Year of Plenty's two cards in either order; both are matched to our engine's form and never change the game. The 139 entry was the one real gap (finding 1, fixed in 672cbcd).
