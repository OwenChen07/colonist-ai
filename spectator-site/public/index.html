    Each round plays self-play games, trains a new net on them, then matches it against the best net so far. It is promoted (and the bots switch to it) only if it is clearly better there (about 52%), and isn't worse against a fixed older net, the AlphaBeta bot, or at judging real games.

    People model

    Every hour a copy of the best net is retrained on people's games to predict the moves people make. It replaces the net in use if it predicts held-out people's moves at least as well. The bot playing it expects its opponent to move like a person.
